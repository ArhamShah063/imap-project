Purpose of visit

I’m Arham Shah, a third-year MBA Tech student specialising in data science at NMIMS in Mumbai. I like taking messy things, like a 35-column Excel tracker, a PNG image or a list of earthquakes, and turning them into something people can actually use.

In 2026 I interned with the Engineering, Procurement & Construction team at Reliance Retail in Navi Mumbai, where two of the projects in this passport were born. I also like civil engineering and construction, so seeing how real stores get built was a good fit. The mix of business and code is exactly why I picked MBA Tech.

Away from a screen I’m planning the next trip, cooking, eating, gaming, or in the water. I’ve jumped out of a plane, been towed into the sky behind a boat, and stood up on a surfboard (briefly). I also care about giving back, and make time for community service.
